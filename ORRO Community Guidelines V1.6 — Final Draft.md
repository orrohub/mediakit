ORRO Community Guidelines V1.6 — Final Draft
==============================

ORRO is built on a foundation of free and open creative expression, which we protect and uphold wherever possible. To maintain a safe environment, we adhere to internationally recognized standards for platform moderation and administration.

### Respect each other

1. Do not promote, coordinate, or engage in harassment. We do not allow any type of harassing behavior, including sexual harassment, ban or block evasion, or coordinating activities that can result in an unfair advantage or outcome.
2. Do not threaten to harm another individual or group of people. This includes direct, indirect, and suggestive threats.
3. Do not share or threaten to share the personally identifiable information (PII) of an individual without consent. This includes providing services that facilitate doxxing, such as buying or selling doxxes or compiling doxxes for others.
4. Do not use hate speech or engage in other hateful conduct. This includes the use of hate symbols, imagery, and claims that deny the history of mass human atrocities. We consider hate speech to be any form of expression that either attacks other people or promotes hatred or violence against them based on their protected characteristics.
5. Do not organize, promote, or support violent extremism. This also includes coordinating violent acts; glorifying or promoting violence or the perpetrators of violent acts; and promoting conspiracy theories that could encourage or incite violence against others.
6. Do not create, post, solicit, share, or make attempts to distribute content on ORRO that depicts, promotes, or attempts to normalize child sexual abuse. This includes manipulated, non-manipulated, or synthetic (artificial intelligence-generated) media involving real, fictional, and/or anthropomorphized children. This also includes animation, drawing, text, and technical commands or prompts to create media depicting child sexual abuse or child sexualization. Any activity of this nature will be reported to local and international authorities.
7. You must be 16 years of age or older to register an account or log in to the application, regardless of your location. If you are under the age of 18, you may not engage in sexual conduct or any other conduct using our service that puts your online or physical safety at risk. This includes consensual sexual interactions between you and other teens of a similar age, as well as any encouragement or coordination of behavior that may be harmful for you or others, such as vigilantism.
8. Do not engage in any sexual conduct with anyone under the age of 18. This includes soliciting, sharing, or exchanging sexual content. We report child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and grooming to the [National Center for Missing & Exploited Children](https://www.missingkids.org/), which may subsequently work with local law enforcement.
9. Do not make sexually explicit content available to anyone under the age of 18. ORRO users must be aged 18 or older to view, gain access to, or participate in adult sexual content on ORRO, and member groups and hashtags are filtered for adult content rigorously.
10. Do not share, distribute, or create sexually explicit or sexually suggestive content of other people over the age of 18 without the subject’s explicit consent. This includes the non-consensual distribution of intimate media that was created either with or without an individual’s consent.
11. Do not share content that glorifies, promotes, or normalizes suicide or other acts of physical self-harm. This includes content that encourages others to cut, burn, or starve themselves, as well as content that normalizes eating disorders, such as anorexia and bulimia. Self-harm acts or threats used as a form of emotional manipulation or coercion are also prohibited.
12. Do not upload or share any material depicting violence, gore, or animal cruelty, especially with the intention to harass or shock others.

### Respect ORRO

13. Do not send unsolicited bulk messages (or spam) to others. Also, do not facilitate this activity, such as by selling spambots, content manipulation tools, account-creation tools, token generators, CAPTCHA-solving services, or other spam tools.

14. Do not use self-bots or user-bots. Each account must be associated with a human, not a bot.

15. Do not engage with our service in an inauthentic way. This includes artificially inflating membership, manipulating engagement metrics, and selling artificial engagement services.

16. Do not sell or purchase ORRO assets, including accounts, usernames, wallets, or custom Group and API links.

17. Do not share false or misleading information (otherwise known as misinformation). Content that is false, misleading, and can lead to significant risk of physical or societal harm may not be shared on ORRO. We may remove content if we reasonably believe its spread could result in damage to physical infrastructure, injury of others, obstruction of participation in civic processes, or the endangerment of public health.

18. Do not misrepresent your identity on ORRO in a deceptive or harmful way. This includes creating fake profiles and attempts to impersonate an individual, group, or organization.

19. Do not evade long-term enforcement actions. This includes creating new or using existing accounts or communities after being removed for violating our platform rules.

20. Do not engage in activities that could damage or compromise the security of an account, network, or system. This includes using deceptive techniques to trick others into revealing sensitive information (phishing), using malicious software (malware), and flooding a target with traffic in order to make a resource unavailable (denial-of-service attacks).

21. Do not use or attempt to use ORRO to promote, coordinate, or execute financial scams. A financial scam is any intentionally deceptive act taken with the intent to receive an illegal, unethical, or otherwise dishonest gain.

22. Do not engage in activities that fraudulently generate a profit at the expense of others. This includes facilitating, providing instructions for, and participating in fraud. We do not allow coordinated efforts to defraud businesses, price gouging, forgery, money laundering, counterfeit goods, or tools that facilitate illegal behavior.

23. Don’t make repetitive or irrelevant comments. Comments should be authentic and original. We also ask that you be kind to others by keeping your commentary respectful and constructive.

24. Do not submit false, misleading, or abusive reports or requests to ORRO’s support teams. A report or request will be considered false or misleading if it contains demonstrably untrue information submitted with the intent to manipulate staff into taking a desired action.

### Follow applicable laws

As described in our Terms of Service, you must comply with all applicable laws, rules, and regulations. This includes:

25. Do not share content that violates anyone's intellectual property or other rights. For more information on how ORRO handles copyright complaints, please view our [Copyright & Intellectual Property Policy](https://orro.ai/copyright/)

26. Do not claim authorship of artwork, writing, or other creative work that is not your own. Uploading another creator's work and presenting it as your own original item is a trust and authenticity violation distinct from a copyright complaint, and may be removed and actioned even where no formal copyright claim has been filed.

27. Do not misuse or misappropriate the services we provide for building trust, reaching consensus, and agreement in the ORRO community. For more information about this please view our [Trust & Authenticity Policy](https://orro.ai/authenticity/).

28. Do not organize, promote, or engage in the selling or facilitation of sales of regulated or potentially dangerous goods. Regulated goods have laws in place that restrict the purchase, sale, trade, or ownership of the goods. Dangerous goods have a reasonable potential to cause real-world, physical harm to individuals.

29. Don't operate multiple accounts or coordinate with other people with the purpose of manipulating the ORRO platform or the Solana blockchain.

30. Don’t use any other unscrupulous tactics to attempt to improperly influence distribution, clicks, or other metrics, including: buying or selling engagement, "overusing" irrelevant keywords, generating inauthentic traffic, or redirecting existing members to new or invalid destinations.

31. Do not coordinate or participate in the illegal gambling, transfer, or trade of ORRO produced tokens. Users are responsible for complying with applicable gambling, transfer, and trade laws and regulation.

32. Do not organize, promote, or engage in any illegal activity that harms the dignity, safety, or wellbeing of another person. This includes but is not limited to human trafficking and sexual solicitation.

Please note that community guidelines are strictly enforced: repeated infractions result in progressive disciplinary action, ranging from 24-hour temporary suspensions to 30-day or 365-day bans for severe or ongoing violations.

If you encounter any content or activity that violates our [Community Guidelines](https://orro.ai/guidelines/), [Terms of Service](https://orro.ai/tos/), or related policies, please report it to our team via our [Support Contact Page](https://orro.ai/help/contact.html?page=guidelines). Please note that we strongly discourage and may take action against vigilantism, as unauthorized enforcement puts individuals at risk and can interfere with official investigations or law enforcement proceedings.

These policies will continue to evolve over time. Consequently, ORRO reserves the right to take action against accounts, spaces, or content that violate the spirit of these frameworks when addressing emerging threats or harms not explicitly detailed in the current version.

We will make every reasonable effort to notify our community when updates occur. Ultimately, maintaining a secure and creative environment is a shared responsibility. Thank you for doing your part to keep ORRO a safe hub for original ideas and meaningful connections.
